📖 AutoQuiz User Manual & Workflow Guide (SRS 2.6)

1. Uploading Materials (BR-1)

Upload lecture slides or notes in PDF, TXT, or Markdown. Content is parsed locally using AST code and LaTeX equation extractors, then vectorized into Chroma DB for grounded question retrieval.

2. Adaptive Remediation Loop (BR-2)

AutoQuiz evaluates readiness objectively. You must score 100% on a topic to master it. If you miss questions, the system isolates your weak concepts, generates newly framed questions, and loops until you achieve 100%.

3. Anti-Guessing Confidence Guard (BR-6)

For every answer, tag your confidence (High, Medium, or Low). Correct answers tagged with Low confidence are marked as Unverified and re-introduced in subsequent loops to prevent bypassing weak points by lucky guesses.

4. Spaced Repetition Memory Decay (BR-5)

Topics degrade after 7-14 days based on the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve. A quick 2-question verification loop upon login restores mastery or initiates a refresh loop.
