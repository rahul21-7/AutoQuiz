📖 AutoQuiz User Manual & Guide (SRS 2.6)

1. Document Ingestion & Local Privacy (BR-1)

Upload your lecture notes in PDF, Markdown (.md), or Plain Text (.txt). All parsing, AST chunking, and embedding vectors are processed and stored locally in Chroma DB to guarantee student privacy.

2. Adaptive 100% Remediation Loop (BR-2)

AutoQuiz operates on an active recall model. Curriculum topics unlock sequentially. Advancement to subsequent modules strictly requires achieving a 100% mastery score on the current loop iteration.

3. Anti-Guessing Confidence Guard (BR-6)

Every answer submission requires selecting your confidence rating: High, Medium, or Low. If an answer is correct but marked with Low confidence, it is flagged as Unverified and earns 0 mastery credit. The concept will be re-introduced in subsequent loops with modified framing to ensure true comprehension.

4. Spaced Repetition Memory Decay (BR-5)

Mastered topics degrade according to an Ebbinghaus retention curve over 7 to 14 days, dynamically scaling based on historical attempt stability. When a topic is marked Review Due, a 2-question micro-verification loop is triggered. A 100% score restores mastery, while failure revokes mastery and initiates full remediation.

5. Interpreting the Weakness Heatmap (BR-4)

The analytics dashboard separates your first-attempt score from looped remediation scores. The Weakness Heatmap color-codes topics by failure rate and unverified lucky guesses, highlighting exact concepts that require review before exams.
