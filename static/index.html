AutoQuiz Workflow & Rules Guide

1. Document Ingestion (BR-1)

Upload lecture slides or notes in PDF, PPTX, TXT, or Markdown. The system parses AST code blocks and LaTeX equations locally, embedding them into local vector storage for grounded question synthesis.

2. Adaptive Remediation Loop (BR-2)

Advancement requires a strict 100% score on the current loop iteration. If you miss questions, AutoQuiz isolates your weak points, frames brand new grounded questions, and loops until complete mastery.

3. Anti-Guessing Confidence Guard (BR-6)

For every answer, tag your confidence (High, Medium, or Low). Correct answers tagged with Low confidence are marked Unverified and re-tested in subsequent loops to prevent bypassing weak points with lucky guesses.
